## 01-wombat-cubes — The Tiny Cube Factory

The study concerns common/bare-nosed wombats. The passage separates the supported formation mechanism from the still-unsettled evolutionary explanation; it does not claim that preventing rolling is the proven purpose.

- University of Tasmania: [Scientists get to the bottom of wombat cubed poo mystery](https://www.utas.edu.au/about/news-and-stories/articles/2021/1109-scientists-get-to-the-bottom-of-wombat-cubed-poo-mystery)

## 02-octopus-taste — Dinner at Your Fingertips

Uses the documented taste-by-touch mechanism and chemical detection in suckers. Does not call arms tentacles, claim literal human taste, or assert that each arm has an independent brain.

- Harvard University: [Touch and taste? It’s all in the suckers](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2020/10/octopus-suction-cups-hold-its-taste-and-touch-sensors/)

## 03-venus-calendar — Venus Is in No Hurry

Explicitly distinguishes the approximately 243-Earth-day sidereal rotation period from a solar day. Avoids the misleading unqualified claim that sunrise-to-sunrise exceeds a Venus year.

- NASA: [Venus: Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/venus/venus-facts/)
- NASA Goddard Space Flight Center: [StarChild: The planet Venus](https://starchild.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/StarChild/solar_system_level2/venus.html)
- NASA Technical Reports Server: [Orbit and Rotation of Venus](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19780020162/downloads/19780020162.pdf?attachment=true)

## 04-eiffel-stretch — A Tower That Stretches

Official operator confirms thermal expansion, cooling contraction, solar tilt, and their imperceptibility to observers. No numerical height change is asserted because the page uses inconsistent millimeter/centimeter wording. Last sentence is an obvious metaphor for thermal expansion, not a biological claim.

- Société d’Exploitation de la Tour Eiffel: [Why does the Eiffel Tower change size?](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/news/history-and-culture/why-does-eiffel-tower-change-size)

## 07-popcorn-pressure — A Snack Under Pressure

Purdue food scientist supports moisture, strong hull, pressure, starch expansion, and one cause of failed popping. Uses “one possible reason,” not a universal explanation. No unsupported health claim or exact temperature.

- Purdue University College of Agriculture: [What’s under the shell of this popular snack?](https://ag.purdue.edu/news/2020/02/whats-under-the-shell-of-this-popular-snack.html)

## 08-flamingo-colors — Wearing Your Lunch

Zoo animal-care source supports dietary carotenoids, algae and brine shrimp, inverted bill, tongue pumping, and filtering structures. Avoids saying all flamingo species eat identical diets or that eating any pink food causes pink feathers.

- Smithsonian’s National Zoo and Conservation Biology Institute: [Why Are Flamingos Pink? And Other Flamingo Facts](https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/news/why-are-flamingos-pink-and-other-flamingo-facts)

## 09-mars-sunset — The Red Planet’s Blue Goodbye

NASA image explanation supports dust-driven blue light scattering close to the Sun, stronger at sunrise/sunset. Explicitly limits the blue region; does not claim the entire sky or every sunset is blue.

- NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory: [Color Variations in the Sky at Sunset](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia00917-color-variations-in-the-sky-at-sunset/)
- NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory: [Mars Movie: I’m Dreaming of a Blue Sunset](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/mars-movie-im-dreaming-of-a-blue-sunset/)

## 10-roman-concrete — Concrete with a Repair Kit
